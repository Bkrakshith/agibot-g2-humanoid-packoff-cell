## Light intensity reasoning

Assumption: Isaac Sim RTX treats a UsdLux RectLight with `normalize=false` as a Lambertian emitter of
radiance `intensity * 2^exposure` in nits (cd/m²), with the 5000 K blackbody colour normalised to
luminance 1. This is the UsdLux definition; RTX is close to it but not exactly calibrated, and its
auto-exposure / tonemapper changes the picture anyway — treat the numbers as relative.

- Real 1200 x 300 LED panel ≈ 3000–3600 lm. Lambertian flux = π·L·A, A = 0.36 m² → L ≈ 2700 nits ↔ 3050 lm.
- Direct illuminance at the work surface (Z 0.85, 3.12 m below) summed over the 25 fittings,
  E = Σ L·dA·cos²θ / r²: **≈ 0.216 lux per nit** → 2700 nits ≈ 585 lux.
- Fill 8 x 8 m at 20 nits adds ≈ 42 lux (stands in for bounce off the white walls/ceiling that a
  real-time renderer under-represents).
- Total ≈ 600–625 lux at the robot, conveyor (Y ±1.8) and rack (checked in `test_room.py`,
  target 500–750 lux); ≈ 370 lux in the corners.

`--light-scale S` (constant `LIGHT_SCALE` in `build.py`) multiplies every light; lux scales linearly.
For randomisation, edit `inputs:intensity` / `inputs:colorTemperature` on `/Room/Lights/Troffer_NN`.

## Brighter hall (2026-10-11, user request)

Defaults changed after a live frame looked too dim: `LIGHT_SCALE` **2.2** (was 1.0) on every light and the fill
RectLight **40 nits** before scaling (was 20; 88 nits after scaling) to lift the corners. Computed direct
illuminance at the work surface (Z 0.85, `test_room.py`): ≈1470 lux at the robot, ≈1430 lux on the
conveyor, ≈760 lux in the far corner (-3.5, -4). The test band is now 1100–1700 lux. RTX auto-exposure
compresses this, so judge brightness by eye in the free and head-camera stills (`output/renders/g2cell_packing_*`).
